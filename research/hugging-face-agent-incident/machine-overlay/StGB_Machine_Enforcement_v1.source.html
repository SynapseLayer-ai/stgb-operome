StGB Machine Enforcement Operome v1

Source-derived policy review · policy version 2026-08-08 · external legal substrate stgb-operome-2026-06-10 · governing language English
Authority boundary. This is a machine-control policy overlay, not German criminal law and not a determination that an AI is criminally liable. The source-derived StGB substrate remains unchanged. The overlay asks whether a proposed machine action materialises selected conduct, circumstance or effect elements and whether execution is authorised.
Terminology. The overlay does not attribute legal intent to a machine. A raw goal is retained for provenance but is not parsed. Structured goal or action-plan facts may supply only material conduct and circumstance facts. Human subjective legal predicates remain in the external StGB substrate and are marked not_assessed_for_machine.
Derivation. This marked source file is the source of truth. The XSD is generated from section, rule, profile and substrate-register attributes in this HTML. The final review HTML adds an XSD-only reconstruction annex.
Machine evaluation boundary
ME-BOUNDARY · ProposedMachineAction isnot null

For every proposed machine action, the harness shall preserve two distinct results. The legal result applies the source-derived StGB actor gate and does not treat a machine as a natural person. The material result evaluates the selected conduct, circumstance and effect elements without that actor gate. Neither result states that the machine is criminally liable.

«if {ProposedMachineAction} isnot null»

{MachineControlHarness} keeps the machine outside the natural-person actor gate; {ActorIsNaturalPerson} is false.

{LegalAttributionResult} remains separate from {MaterialEvaluationResult} for {SelectedStGBSection}.

«end if»

Goal and observation evidence
ME-EVIDENCE · ProposedMachineAction isnot null

The harness shall retain the raw machine goal for provenance and shall not parse it as legal or material evidence. Exact structured facts derived from a goal or action plan may fill only declared non-subjective elements of the selected section. Trusted observed facts take precedence. A conflict with observation or a fact outside the selected section contract blocks execution.

«if {ProposedMachineAction} isnot null»

{MachineControlHarness} retains {RawMachineGoal} from {GoalEvidenceSource} and does not parse the raw goal; {RawGoalParsed} is false.

{StructuredGoalFacts} may fill declared material elements; {TrustedObservedFacts} prevail where {ObservedFactPrecedenceApplied} is true. {GoalFactConflict} and {GoalFactOutsideSelectedSectionContract} feed [MachineActionBlocked].

«end if»

Human subjective legal predicates
ME-SUBJECTIVE · SubjectiveElementStatus is "not_assessed_for_machine"

A machine goal, action plan or observation shall not satisfy a human subjective legal predicate. Such predicates remain part of the source-derived legal substrate. Their machine status is not_assessed_for_machine, supplied values are ignored, and a pivotal subjective predicate escalates as non-executable.

«if {SubjectiveElementStatus} is "not_assessed_for_machine"»

{MachineControlHarness} does not assess human subjective legal predicates for a machine. {SubjectiveElementsPresent} and {SubjectiveValuesSuppliedButIgnored} are reported; {SubjectiveElementsPivotal} feeds [MachineActionEscalated].

«end if»

External authorization
ME-AUTHORIZATION · ProposedMachineAction isnot null

A machine or public caller shall not verify its own authorization. Positive authorization permits execution only when a trusted external capability broker verifies the authority artifact for the current run and scope. Negative authorization blocks. Unknown or unverified positive authorization escalates as non-executable.

«if {ProposedMachineAction} isnot null»

{MachineControlHarness} accepts verified authority only from an external capability broker. {AuthorizationDecision}, {AuthorizationVerified}, {AuthorizationSource} and {AuthorizationScope} identify the authority evidence from {ExternalCapabilityBroker}.

«end if»

Blocking decision
ME-BLOCK · MachineActionBlocked is true

The harness shall block execution when material elements are realised, authorization is negative, structured goal evidence conflicts with trusted observation, or structured goal evidence lies outside the selected section contract. A blocked action is not executable.

«if [MachineActionBlocked] is true»

{MachineControlHarness} prevents execution when {GoalFactConflict}, {GoalFactOutsideSelectedSectionContract}, {AuthorizationDecision} or {MaterialEvaluationResult} causes [MachineActionBlocked]; [MachineActionExecutable] is false.

«end if»

Escalation decision
ME-ESCALATE · MachineActionEscalated is true

The harness shall escalate when pivotal material facts remain unknown, a human subjective predicate is pivotal, authorization is unknown, or positive authorization has not been externally verified. Escalation is non-executable until the missing evidence is resolved.

«if [MachineActionEscalated] is true»

{MachineControlHarness} prevents execution pending resolution when {MaterialEvaluationResult}, {SubjectiveElementsPivotal}, {AuthorizationDecision} or {AuthorizationVerified} causes [MachineActionEscalated]; [MachineActionExecutable] is false.

«end if»

Allow decision
ME-ALLOW · MachineActionAllowed is true

The harness may allow execution only when the material result is determined not realised, positive authorization is externally verified, and neither a block nor an escalation applies. Allow is the only executable decision.

«if [MachineActionAllowed] is true»

{MachineControlHarness} permits execution only where {MaterialEvaluationResult} is determined negative, {AuthorizationDecision} is positive, {AuthorizationVerified} is true, and [MachineActionBlocked] and [MachineActionEscalated] are false; [MachineActionExecutable] is true.

«end if»
