§232 · Trafficking in human beings

Source (English, unofficial)
Operome markup - original text encoded
(1) A person who, exploiting another person’s personal or financial predicament or their helplessness associated with their stay in a foreign country, recruits, transports, passes on, accommodates or takes in that person, or who recruits, transports, passes on, accommodates or takes in another person under the age of twenty-one, shall be punished with imprisonment for a term of six months to five years if 1. that person is to be exploited a) in the practice of prostitution or in the performance of sexual acts on or in front of the perpetrator or a third person, or in the submission to sexual acts on themselves by the perpetrator or a third person, b) through employment, c) in the practice of begging, or d) in the commission by that person of acts punishable by law, 2. that person is to be held in slavery, serfdom, debt bondage or in conditions equivalent or similar thereto, or 3. that person is to have an organ unlawfully removed. Exploitation through employment within the meaning of sentence 1, point 1(b) occurs where, driven by a reckless pursuit of profit, the employment takes place under working conditions that are grossly disproportionate to the working conditions of employees engaged in the same or a comparable occupation (exploitative employment). (2) A person who, with the intention of exploiting another person in the manner described in paragraph 1, sentence 1, points 1 to 3, 1. recruits, transports, transfers, harbours or accommodates that person by means of violence, threats of serious harm or deception, or 2. abducts or seizes that person, or facilitates their seizure by a third party. (3) In the cases referred to in paragraph 1, a term of imprisonment of between six months and ten years shall be imposed if 1. the victim is under the age of eighteen at the time of the offence, 2. the offender physically abuses the victim severely during the offence, or, through the offence or an act committed during the offence, at least recklessly exposes the victim to the risk of death or serious harm to health, or 3. the offender acts professionally or as a member of a gang that has joined together for the purpose of continuously committing such offences. In the cases referred to in paragraph 2, a term of imprisonment of between one and ten years shall be imposed if any of the circumstances referred to in sentence 1, points 1 to 3, apply. (4) In the cases referred to in paragraphs 1, 2 and 3, first sentence, an attempt is punishable.

(1) A person who, exploiting another person’s personal or financial predicament or their helplessness associated with their stay in a foreign country, induces that person, or who induces another person under the age of twenty-one, 1. to take up or continue prostitution, or 2. to perform sexual acts, through which they are exploited, on or in front of the offender or a third person, or to allow the offender or a third person to perform such acts on them, shall be punished with imprisonment for a term of six months to ten years. (2) An attempt to commit this offence is punishable. (3) A person who, by means of violence, the threat of serious harm or deceit, induces another person to take up or continue prostitution or to perform the sexual acts referred to in paragraph 1, point 2, shall be liable to imprisonment for a term of between one and ten years. (4) In the cases referred to in paragraph 1, a term of imprisonment of between one and ten years shall be imposed, and in the cases referred to in paragraph 3, a term of imprisonment of not less than one year shall be imposed, if any of the circumstances referred to in Section 232(3), first sentence, points 1 to 3, are present. (5) In less serious cases under paragraph 1, a term of imprisonment of between three months and five years shall be imposed; in less serious cases under paragraphs 3 and 4, a term of imprisonment of between six months and ten years shall be imposed. (6) A person who, in respect of a person who has become the victim 1. of human trafficking under Section 232(1), first sentence, point 1(a), including in conjunction with Section 232(2), or 2. of an offence under paragraphs 1 to 5, and who is engaged in prostitution, performs sexual acts on that person for remuneration or has that person perform such acts on the offender, thereby exploiting that person’s personal or financial predicament or their helplessness associated with their stay in a foreign country, shall be punished with imprisonment for a term of three months to five years. If, during the sexual act, the offender is at least recklessly unaware of the circumstances referred to in the first sentence, number 1 or 2, or of the victim’s personal or financial predicament or helplessness, the penalty shall be imprisonment for up to three years or a fine. No penalty shall be imposed under the first and second sentences on a person who, in respect of an act under the first sentence, point 1 or 2, committed to the detriment of a person engaged in prostitution under the first sentence, voluntarily reports such an offence to the competent authority or voluntarily causes such a report to be made, unless the offence had already been discovered in whole or in part at that time and the offender knew this or, on reasonable assessment of the circumstances, should have foreseen it.

§233 · Exploitation of labour

ExploitationOfLabour · provenance StGB 233 · (1) and (5) encoded; (2)-(4) struck
Source (English, unofficial)
Operome markup - original text encoded
(1) A person who exploits another person, taking advantage of their personal or financial predicament or their helplessness associated with their stay in a foreign country, or who exploits another person under the age of twenty-one, 1. through employment as defined in Section 232(1), second sentence, 2. in the practice of begging, or 3. in the commission by that person of acts punishable by law, shall be punished with imprisonment for a term not exceeding three years or a fine. (2) A term of imprisonment of between six months and ten years shall be imposed if 1. the victim is under the age of eighteen at the time of the offence, 2. the offender physically abuses the victim severely during the offence or, through the offence or an act committed during the offence, at least recklessly exposes the victim to the risk of death or serious harm to health, 3. the perpetrator places the victim in financial distress by wholly or partially withholding the remuneration customary for the victim’s work, or significantly exacerbates an existing financial distress, or 4. the perpetrator acts as a member of a gang which has formed with the intention of committing such offences on a continuous basis. (3) An attempt is punishable. (4) In less serious cases under paragraph 1, the penalty shall be imprisonment for up to two years or a fine; in less serious cases under paragraph 2, the penalty shall be imprisonment for a term of three months to five years. (5) A person who facilitates an offence under paragraph 1(1) by 1. arranging exploitative employment (Section 232(1), second sentence), 2. letting business premises, or 3. letting residential premises to the person to be exploited, shall be punished with imprisonment for up to two years or a fine. Sentence 1 shall not apply if the offence is already punishable by a more severe penalty under other provisions.
